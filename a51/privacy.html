Rami 51 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Rami 51 mobile game (the "app") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Glarekon, an independent game developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at glarekonstudio@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The app uses Google services to show ads (AdMob), count how the game is played (Firebase Analytics), report crashes (Firebase Crashlytics) and adjust game settings (Firebase Remote Config).
  • Where the law requires it, you choose whether ads can be personalised and whether Analytics runs. You can change your mind any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • We don't sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (sound, vibration, language, card style and similar options), your last match setup, and whether you've finished the tutorial. This stays on your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

2. Information collected by Google services

The app uses the following services from Google. They collect the information below on our behalf, or, for ads, as an independent company.

Google AdMob (ads)

To show ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address, information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires consent, ads are only personalised to your interests if you agree. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which still use limited data, for example your approximate location (from your IP address), to limit how often an ad repeats and to prevent fraud. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Firebase Analytics (game statistics)

Analytics tells us how the game is played so we can improve it. It records events such as starting and finishing a match, how a round ended, your finishing place, the number of players and rounds, finishing or skipping the tutorial, and the ads shown along with the revenue they earned. It also collects an app instance ID, your device model, operating system and app version, your language, your approximate location (country or region, from your IP address) and how long you use the app. It may also collect your advertising ID.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Analytics stays off unless you agree in the consent message.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report: what went wrong in the code, your device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation ID. Crash reports don't include your settings or any personal content.

Firebase Remote Config (game settings)

The app downloads settings from Firebase, such as the turn timer or how often ads may appear, so we can adjust the game without an update. To do this, it sends a random installation ID, the app version, and your device's language and country.

Learn more: Privacy and security in Firebase and Google's privacy policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent message: if you're in a region where consent is required, the app asks you on first launch before showing ads or turning Analytics on. "Do not consent" is always an option.
  • Privacy options: open Settings → Privacy options in the app at any time to review or change your choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection and deletes the data stored on your device.

5. How long information is kept

  • Analytics data linked to an app instance: up to 14 months. Summary reports with no individual data may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Data on your device: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We only share information with the Google services listed above. We don't sell your information. Google may process it on servers outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict how it's used, or withdraw your consent. Email us at glarekonstudio@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we usually can't tell which data belongs to you, but we'll tell you what we can do. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

US state privacy laws: under some US state laws, showing personalised ads can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or as "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

8. Children

Rami 51 is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent where they live. If you believe a child's information was collected, contact us and we'll help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. Online play is planned for a future version. If it adds accounts or new kinds of data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

10. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).
