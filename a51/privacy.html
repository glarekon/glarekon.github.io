A51 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect information in connection with the A51 mobile game. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information collected automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • device information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language settings;
  • your device's advertising identifier and other device or installation identifiers;
  • approximate location at country or region level, derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you use the App, such as matches played, game results and ads viewed;
  • technical information about errors and crashes.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Playing offline does not require an account.

Online play

If you play online, the App creates a guest account for you on our game server. You do not need to sign up or give an email address or password. For online play, we collect and store:

  • a random identifier created by the App, which signs your device in to your account;
  • the display name you choose. It is shown to other players and must be unique, and names containing offensive words are refused;
  • your online match results and statistics, such as matches played, wins and average place, which other players can see on your profile;
  • your friends, your friend requests and the players you block. Your friends can see when you are online or in a match;
  • reports you send about other players, or that other players send about you, with the reason chosen and the match concerned;
  • your IP address, which is needed to connect your device to our server and to protect it from abuse.

During a match you can send preset quick-chat lines and emotes. They are shown to the other players at the table and are not stored. The App has no free-text chat.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, and sends our server the identifier Firebase gives your installation of the App. We use it only to tell you when a friend invites you to their room while the App is closed. If you do not allow notifications, nothing is registered. You can turn friend invites off at any time in the App, under Settings → Notifications, and our server then deletes this identifier. You can also turn notifications off in your device settings.

Information you provide

If you contact us or send feedback through the App, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, along with your email address. See Feedback below.

Information stored on your device

Your game settings and preferences are stored only on your device. We do not have access to them, and they are deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate and maintain the App;
  • provide online play: matches, your profile, friends and invitations from them;
  • keep online play safe, by refusing offensive names, limiting how often requests and invitations can be sent, and reviewing reports;
  • display advertising, which allows us to offer the App free of charge;
  • understand how the App is used, so we can improve the game and its balance;
  • detect, diagnose and fix technical problems;
  • adjust game settings without requiring an update;
  • respond to your messages and feedback;
  • prevent fraud and comply with our legal obligations.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised to your interests. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalised ads, which are based on general information such as your approximate location and the content of the App rather than your personal interests. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Feedback

The App lets you send us feedback by email. When you choose this option, the App opens your own email application with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. The draft includes your app version, operating system version, device model and language to help us investigate problems. You can edit or delete this information before sending.

We use feedback only to respond to you and to improve the App. We keep feedback emails for as long as needed for those purposes and then delete them.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with other players, if you play online: your display name and profile statistics, and, for your friends only, whether you are online or in a match;
  • with Google, which provides the advertising, usage analytics, crash reporting, configuration and notification services used by the App, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy;
  • with the hosting and network providers that run and connect our game server, which process information only on our behalf;
  • when required by law or to protect our rights, our users or others.

6. Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in a region where consent is required, the App asks for your choices the first time you open it, before personalised ads or usage analytics are used. You can decline.
  • Privacy options: you can review or change your choices at any time in the App, under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising identifier: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Notifications: you can turn friend invites on or off at any time in the App, under Settings → Notifications, or turn all notifications off in your device settings.
  • Blocking and reporting: you can block or report another player from the Friends page or during a match.
  • Deleting your online account: under Settings → Account → Delete account, you can delete your online account and the information stored with it on our server, including your display name, statistics, friends and notification identifier. Reports are kept as described in section 8.
  • Uninstalling: uninstalling the App stops all further collection and removes the information stored on your device. It does not delete your online account: delete it in the App first, or contact us with your display name and we will delete it.

7. Legal bases for processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your information on the following legal bases:

  • Consent, for personalised advertising and usage analytics. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Consent, for notifications, which you give through your device's permission prompt and can withdraw in the App or in your device settings.
  • Performance of a contract, for online play you choose to use: your account, display name, matches, statistics, friends and invitations.
  • Legitimate interests, for non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention, crash reporting and game configuration, which allow us to provide a free, stable and well-balanced game.
  • Legitimate interests, for keeping online play safe: refusing offensive names, limiting requests and invitations, and reviewing reports.
  • Legitimate interests, for responding to feedback you choose to send us.

8. Data retention

Usage analytics are retained for up to 14 months, and crash reports for up to 90 days. Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with its own policies. Feedback emails are kept as described in section 4. Information stored on your device remains there until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

Your online account and the information stored with it are kept until you delete your account. Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them and keep online play safe, and for no longer than 12 months, including after the account of the player who sent or received them is deleted.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. For online play, include your display name so we can find your account; you can also delete your account yourself in the App. Offline play does not use accounts, so we may be unable to link other information to you, but we will respond to every request and explain what we can do. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

United States: under some US state privacy laws, personalised advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time in the App, under Settings → Privacy options.

11. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the applicable age of consent in their country. Online play shows other players only display names and preset chat lines; there is no free-text chat. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. If we make significant changes, such as collecting new types of information, we will update this policy before those changes take effect.
