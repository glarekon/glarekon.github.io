Delete your A51 account

A51, a card game by Glarekon. Last updated: 7 November 2026

You only have an account in A51 if you have played online. Offline play against bots creates no account. You can delete your online account at any time, in the game or by email.

In the game

  1. Open A51 and tap the Settings gear at the top of the screen.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email, if you no longer have the game

Send an email to glarekonstudio@gmail.com with the subject Delete my A51 account, and include your display name. If you linked a Google account, send it from that Google account's email address, or tell us which one it is. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your display name, which becomes free for others to use;
  • your match statistics, level, progress and daily rewards;
  • your coins and the virtual items you own or wear;
  • your friends, friend requests and the players you blocked;
  • your notification identifier;
  • the linked Google account's identifier and email address.

Deleting cannot be undone. Coins and virtual items are not refunded.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports you sent about other players, or that others sent about you, for up to 12 months, to keep online play safe.
  • Purchase records (the product, date and Google Play order details) for as long as needed to handle refunds, prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.
  • Remove ads stays linked to your Google Play account, not to your A51 account, so it still works if you reinstall.

Usage analytics and crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically, after 14 months and 90 days. Settings saved on your phone are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

For more about the information we collect, see our Privacy Policy.
